title: Definition scanning
description: "Turn Open MCP Portal's definition scanning off or keep it in shadow mode, read its rule catalog, and use findings when you review servers."

Open MCP Portal scans every capability definition (tool, prompt, resource and resource template) with local heuristics when it reads a server's capabilities, and shows the findings to the server's team and to reviewers. It is **shadow only**: nothing is blocked, redacted, rewritten or approved because of a finding, and nothing is sent to an outside service. What a finding means, and where teams see them, is in [Definition scan findings](/docs/teams/definition-scanning).

**Admin › Settings › Definition scanning** (`/admin/scanning`) holds the one setting and the rule catalog. Platform admins change the setting; auditors see the page read-only. It needs a browser session.

## The mode

| Mode | Does |
|---|---|
| **Shadow** (the default, also after an upgrade from v0.13) | Scans definitions at every refresh, and in a background pass for definitions stored before. Findings show beside the definitions. |
| **Off** | Scans nothing. Capabilities show "Scanning disabled. No current scan is available." instead of findings. |

There is no mode that blocks or redacts. Changes are saved with the page's revision (if someone else saved first, reload and choose again) and are audited as `settings.scanning_update`.

## The rule catalog

The **Rule catalog** section lists every rule of the ruleset compiled into the portal (`v1`, 32 rules) with its id, group and default severity: the credential shapes of the shared redaction rules (**secret**), Unicode tricks (**unicode**), English phrasing aimed at the model (**poison**) and over-long text (**length**). It is read-only: rules can't be added, switched off or tuned per install. A new ruleset comes with a release, and definitions scanned with an older one show as "not yet scanned under the current definition/ruleset" until the background pass reaches them.

## Using findings in review

When you approve a server or a changed capability, its findings are in the dialog, beside the definition. Treat them as pointers to where to read closely, not as a verdict:

- A **high** finding, such as a credential shape, hidden Unicode or text that tells the model to keep something from the user, is worth asking the team about before you approve.
- **info** and **low** findings (a link, a path, a long description) are often legitimate. Check that they fit what the tool does.
- **No findings** proves nothing. Under **Self-publish** nobody reviews, so findings are only for the team.

**Rescan definitions** in the dialog or on the server's Capabilities tab scans the stored definitions again without contacting the server. See [Catalog review](/docs/administer/catalog-review).

## For operators

Scanning needs no setting outside the web app. Each definition is limited to 64 KiB and a small time budget; a definition over either is recorded as an incomplete scan with its reason, never as clean. Only rule ids, severities, counts and locations are stored; the matched text never is, nor reaches logs, metrics or the audit log. The metrics `omp_scan_findings_total`, `omp_scan_duration_seconds`, `omp_scan_incomplete_total` and `omp_scan_ruleset_info` are listed in [Metrics](/docs/operate/metrics).
